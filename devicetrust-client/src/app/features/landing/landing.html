Three steps to a verified device

1

Register the device

An owner registers a device and gets a unique Passport ID and QR code to attach to it.

2

Repairs get logged

An approved technician scans the device and logs diagnosis, parts, and work done. Once submitted, it's permanent.

3

Anyone can verify it

Scan the QR or enter the Passport ID — no account needed — to see the device's full verified history.
