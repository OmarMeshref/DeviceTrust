About

A device's history shouldn't disappear with its owner.

DeviceTrust exists because buying a used laptop, phone, or tablet is still an act of faith. A seller can say a device was never repaired. A buyer has no way to check.

What it does

Every registered device gets a Passport ID and a QR code. When a technician repairs the device, that record — once submitted — becomes permanent. It can't be quietly edited or deleted, even by the technician who wrote it. Anyone who scans the device later sees the same history, without needing an account.

Why it can be trusted

A repair only counts if it comes from a technician linked to an approved repair centre, checked again at the moment it's submitted — not just when the paperwork started. Ownership changes are tracked the same way: closing one record and opening the next in a single step, so the history never has a gap.
